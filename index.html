What Noah is

Personal agents. Self-hosted.

Noah is software you install on your own server or computer. Your agents live there, and so does everything they know.

What you get

  • Your agents, on your machineConversations, memory and files stay on the server you run.
  • A computer for each agentIts own browser, memory and files. When a login needs you, take over its screen.
  • The model you chooseLocal or in the cloud, with your own key and a spending cap.

What it isn't

  • Not a hosted serviceWe don't run your agents. You do.
  • No account with usNo sign-up, no subscription, no telemetry.
  • Not tied to one modelSwitch providers whenever you like.
What your agents do

Hand it off. Get it back done.

Each agent takes a task from start to finish, and checks with you when a step is yours.

Start from a template

Personal assistant, news watch, research, content, shopping, home finances, travel, learning, app building. Pick one and make it yours.

In your pocket

Chat from the Android app or your phone's browser. Your agents tell you when a task is done, and can call you when they need you.

Bring any model

Plug in your own key, set a spending cap, and give each agent the model that fits the job.

OllamaOpenRouterAnthropicOpenAIGeminiMistralDeepSeekAny OpenAI-compatible API

Works while you don't

Scheduled tasks, sources to watch, goals it keeps in mind. It picks things up on its own and reports back.

  • Every day, 7:00Morning news digest
  • MondaysBudget check-in
  • WatchingFlight prices to Lisbon

Asks before it acts

Sending, posting or anything sensitive shows up as a card first. You approve or decline.

Proof, not promises

Every answer comes with a receipt: what the agent says it did, next to what its tools actually did.

Your tools

Gmail, Calendar, Drive and Contacts, Instagram, X, and any MCP server. You decide what each agent may read or do.

A team, not a chatbot

Agents hand work to each other. Add people with their own profile, and share an agent with the rights you choose.

Updates itself

New versions install at a quiet hour, with a backup first and an automatic rollback if anything breaks.

Get started

Up and running in ten minutes.

Let your coding agent set it up, or run one command on your server.

Easiest

Ask your coding agent

Paste this into Claude Code, Codex or Cursor. It installs Noah on your server, checks it, and gives you the link to create your account.

Install Noah Agent on my VPS by following https://github.com/smfcoder17/noah-agent/blob/main/AGENTS.md
  1. 01Answer two questions: how to reach your server, and which address to use.
  2. 02Open the link it gives you. Your passwords and keys are typed by you, in your browser.
One command

Do it yourself

On an Ubuntu or Debian server, as root:

$ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/smfcoder17/noah-agent/main/get.sh | sh
  1. 01Open the link printed at the end. It works once.
  2. 02Create your account, pick your model and paste its key.
  3. 03Create your first agent from a template.
